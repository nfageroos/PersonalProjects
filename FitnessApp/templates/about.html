About This App

This website helps users understand their daily nutrition needs by calculating personalized calorie and macro targets and letting them track what they eat.

How the Site Works

  • Enter your age, weight, height, sex, activity level, and goal
  • The app calculates your TDEE
  • It generates your recommended daily calories and macros
  • Search for foods and log what you eat
  • Your nutrition totals update based on your logs
